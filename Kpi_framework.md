# E-Commerce KPI Trends and Performance Analysis

Dataset: E-commerce Customer Behaviour Dataset  
Records analysed: 5,000 orders  
Analysis period: January 2023 – March 2024

## 1. Overview

The e-commerce dataset was analysed to understand business performance, customer engagement, sales trends and operational efficiency. Ten Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) were selected and classified into leading and lagging indicators to evaluate both potential future performance signals and actual business outcomes.

## 2. Sales Performance Trends

The business generated total revenue of approximately **4.92 million** from 5,000 orders, with an Average Order Value (AOV) of 983.11 and 11,100 units sold.

Monthly revenue fluctuated throughout the analysis period. January 2024 recorded the highest monthly revenue of approximately 396,875, while April 2023 recorded the lowest revenue of approximately 257,333. Revenue remained relatively strong during the final quarter of 2023 and the first quarter of 2024.

The average order value also showed improvement, reaching approximately 1,064.45 in Q1 2024 compared with 967.51 in Q1 2023. This suggests an increase in the average spending per order.

## 3. Customer Behaviour Trends

Customer engagement indicators showed relatively stable browsing behaviour. The average session duration was 14.57 minutes, while customers viewed an average of 8.98 pages.

Approximately 59.8% of orders were flagged as returning-customer orders. This indicates a substantial share of orders associated with returning customers, although the customer identifiers should be checked before drawing conclusions about actual repeat purchases.

These engagement measures and the returning-customer flag can be monitored as leading performance signals.

## 4. Operational Performance Trends

The average delivery time was 6.50 days, indicating the overall delivery duration across the dataset. The average customer rating was 3.90 out of 5, suggesting generally positive customer feedback.

The average discount rate was approximately 2.47% of gross product value. Monitoring discount rates can help businesses understand their discounting practices and their potential impact on revenue.

## 5. Leading vs Lagging KPI Classification

The analysis classified the selected KPIs into two categories:

- Leading indicators: Returning Customer Share, Average Session Duration and Average Pages Viewed.
- Lagging indicators: Total Revenue, Total Orders, Average Order Value, Total Units Sold, Average Discount Rate, Average Delivery Time and Average Customer Rating.

Leading indicators help monitor customer engagement and potential future business performance, while lagging indicators measure actual business outcomes.

## 6. Conclusion and Recommendations

The analysis indicates that the e-commerce business maintained relatively stable performance, with strong revenue in January 2024 and an improvement in average order value over the quarterly comparison.

The business should focus on improving customer engagement, monitoring returning-customer behaviour, maintaining delivery efficiency and analysing customer feedback.

The Power BI dashboard provides a consolidated view of these KPIs through interactive cards, trend charts and filters, helping management monitor performance and make data-driven decisions.

Note: March 2024 data ends on March 26, so it represents a partial month. The dataset also contains 5,000 unique Customer_ID values for 5,000 orders; therefore, the returning-customer flag should not be treated as independently verified repeat purchasing.
