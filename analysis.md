Executive Summary: Analysis of E-Commerce Customer Behavior

Using an e-commerce dataset with 5,000 transactions and 18 variables from January 2023 to March 2024, this analysis looks at customer purchasing behavior and sales performance. The goal is to assess the relationship between customer engagement, sales, delivery time, and customer happiness while comprehending sales performance across product categories, customer demographics, and device types.

With no missing values in any of the 18 columns, the dataset was full and offered a reliable foundation for the research.
Principal Results

1. Performance by Product Category
   The company made about 4.92 million in total sales. The best-performing category was electronics, which accounted for over 2.33 million (47.4%) of total sales. Sports came in second with 754,563.56, followed by Home & Garden with 908,348.86. The biggest sales potential are found in these three categories.
2. Customer and Device Segmentation
   With over 2.81 million sales, mobile devices led the pack, followed by desktop machines with about 1.65 million and tablets with about 455,000. This suggests that the most significant sales channel is mobile. Electronics consistently performs well across genders and device kinds, according to the data.
3. Sales and Customer Engagement
   Using Pages Viewed, Session Duration, Delivery Time, Customer Rating, and Unit Price, a linear regression model was created to analyze sales. These factors together account for about 63.4% of the variation in sales in the test dataset, according to the model's R-squared value of 0.634. This finding does not, however, prove that longer browsing sessions or more page views on their own boost sales.

4. Customer satisfaction and delivery time
   There was hardly any linear relationship in this dataset, as evidenced by the correlation between delivery time and customer rating of roughly -0.01 . Customer evaluations do not seem to be explained by delivery time alone.
   Three Practical Suggestions

5. Give High-Performing Product Categories Priority
   Boost the accessibility and prominence of products related to electronics, home and garden, and sports. To take advantage of these categories' significant sales contributions, concentrate marketing efforts, promotional offers, and inventory planning on them.

6. Enhance the Mobile Purchase Experience
   Improve mobile website usability, page loading speed, product navigation, and checkout simplicity because mobile devices provide the most sales. To increase conversion rates and customer retention, implement mobile-focused marketing.

7. Boost Customer Involvement and Track Satisfaction
   Examine browsing habits, such as the number of pages viewed and the length of the session, in addition to the actual results of purchases. To promote purchases, use tailored discounts and product recommendations. To find further areas for improvement, keep a close eye on customer ratings, delivery performance, and comments.

Conclusion
According to the data, mobile devices are the best sales channel and electronics are the category that generates the most money. While the delivery-time correlation indicates that a more thorough examination of customer satisfaction is necessary, the regression model offers a helpful beginning point for comprehending sales patterns. By putting the three suggestions into practice, the company can improve its online shopping experience, make better judgments, and spot chances for long-term sales development.
